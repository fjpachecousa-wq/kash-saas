Frequently Asked Questions

The Basics
What is KASH?

KASH is a financial projection and goal-tracking tool. You set a savings goal — an amount and a deadline — and KASH tracks your real progress toward it by syncing with the external account where your money is actually being deposited.

Is KASH a bank or a brokerage?

No. KASH does not open accounts, hold your money, or execute trades. It's not a bank, broker-dealer, or credit union, and it never will be. Your money stays in your own bank or brokerage account — KASH just tracks it against your goal.

Does KASH give investment advice?

No. KASH is not a registered investment advisor and never recommends specific assets. All projections are estimates based on your own inputs, for reference only. See our Financial Disclaimer.

What is "My Goal" / Sync?

My Goal is the core of KASH: a savings target you define, at a pace you choose (daily, weekly, or monthly). Sync is what confronts that plan against the real money actually moving in your connected account, day by day — that's what makes the goal feel real and trackable.

Account & Eligibility
Do I need a brokerage account to use KASH?

You need some external account where your savings already live — a bank, brokerage, or credit union account. It can be a simple savings account; you don't need to hold any investments. If it happens to be a brokerage account, KASH can also show your actual holdings, but that's optional.

Who can use KASH?

KASH is available to users aged 16 and older. Users between 16 and 17 should have the involvement and consent of a parent or legal guardian. See our Terms of Service for full eligibility details.

Pricing & Billing
How much does KASH cost?

KASH Pro is $9.99/month. New subscribers may be offered an introductory rate of $6.99/month for the first 3 months via a promotional code, after which billing continues at the standard $9.99/month.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel at any time from your account settings. Access continues until the end of your current billing cycle — no partial-period refunds are issued. Payment is processed exclusively through Stripe; KASH never sees or stores your card details.

Connections & Data
How does KASH connect to my bank or brokerage?

Through Plaid, a widely used connection service. KASH receives read-only access to your positions and balances for display and comparison only — it cannot move money, execute trades, or access your login credentials.

Can I disconnect my account?

Yes — contact us at kash@kashsolutions.us and we'll disconnect it for you. A self-service disconnect option is planned for a future release.

Is my data safe?

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest via Supabase, and payments are handled exclusively by Stripe — KASH never stores your card details. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
